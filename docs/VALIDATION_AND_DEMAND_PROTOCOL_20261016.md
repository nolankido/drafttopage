# Remaining real-world evidence protocol

Prepared October 16, 2026. All sessions, outreach, traffic comparisons, repeat-use observations and paid commitments described here are **NOT_RUN**. This document completes preparation only. The user has not authorized contacting people, collecting their responses, adding tracking, activating providers, billing or incurring charges.

## One private evidence record

Use [the existing public alpha kit](PUBLIC_ALPHA_STUDY_20261016.md) and its blank session template for the conversion task. Store completed records outside the public repository. Use participant codes rather than names in analysis. Record consent and an agreed deletion date before retaining any session material. Prefer task outcomes and short observations over copies of personal writing or recordings.

For every outcome record the task date, source application/version if known, browser/OS, device, input class, destination version/role/plugins if known, assistance, failure/recovery and exact denominator. “Unknown” is a valid value. Distinguish failure, refusal, lost follow-up and missing evidence. Do not turn an unavailable observation into a success or a zero.

## Device and assistive-technology acceptance

Before testing, the owner selects actual available devices and assistive technology. Do not list a configuration as tested until its whole task is observed. At minimum include a desktop keyboard-only session, an actual desktop screen reader, an actual touch phone, and a mobile assistive-technology session when available. Record exact configurations rather than claiming all-browser support.

Use fictional writing for each task:

1. Reach the matching converter, choose a file or paste a selection, inspect the conversion notices and apply after acknowledgement. Confirm no early replacement.
2. Navigate between article, reading preview, a finding and its exact editor passage. Repair an ordinary link and a table cell using the guided controls. Confirm usable focus, labels, source context and reflow without losing the passage.
3. Approve, copy, then edit while a copy is pending if the device permits it. Check feedback for current/earlier output. Deny clipboard permission once and use the file fallback. Verify actual file bytes and destination receipt separately from the download request.
4. Deliberately opt in to browser recovery after reading its unencrypted local-device disclosure. Reopen, inspect without automatic restore, restore after comparison, verify approval clears, then pause and remove the copy. Check focus after each action and competing-tab behavior. Start a separate default-off session and verify nothing is saved.
5. Save an unpublished destination draft, close/reopen, inspect text and native blocks, edit one paragraph, save/reopen again. Confirm the original ending and no private source/review fields in article output.

Record any unexpected-content warning, silent loss, invisible focus, unavailable control or misleading status as a blocking defect. Preserve a minimal fictional reproduction for engineering. Browser emulation, accessibility-tree extraction and local WordPress fixtures remain separate evidence.

## Product proof and time saving, original item 16

Ask the participant to choose a typical permitted article and their normal handoff method. Record the same start/end boundary for both methods: ready source article to saved/reopened, reviewed unpublished destination draft. Include image repair and cleanup when those are part of the task. Counterbalance method order where practical and record prior practice, assistance, interruptions and failed attempts. Do not compare an easy sample conversion with an unrelated full manual workflow.

Report individual paired outcomes, median change, range and failed-task denominator. A faster failed task is not a saving. Do not make a public numeric time-saving claim until the input population, method, dates and limitations support it and participants permit the use of the evidence. A fictional walkthrough is product demonstration only.

## Search observation, original item 17

The existing converter routes are the landing pages. Keep their URLs stable; troubleshooting links should return to the matching tool. After authorized publication, independently check response, canonical, sitemap inclusion and usability. A native Cloudflare build does not verify indexing or public rendering.

If the owner already has a verified Search Console property, use its read-only report or a deliberately exported report. Do not register a property, alter DNS, submit private URLs or connect another service without authorization. Compare equal dated windows for the two converter routes. Record impressions, clicks, query categories, countries/devices where available, indexing state and missing/anonymized queries. Report clicks as clicks, not completed conversions. Search Console provides no proof of task completion or repeat use. No client analytics or writing telemetry is needed or authorized.

## Distribution experiment, original item 18

After explicit contact authorization, choose one audience, one article workflow and at most two owner-selected channels. An editor or small agency with recurring Word/Google Docs-to-WordPress work is a hypothesis, not a verified customer segment. Prepare a demonstration from the fictional walkthrough and describe known image, checklist and layout limits. Use no invented testimonial or speed claim.

Record authorized placements/messages and dates privately. Use existing aggregate channel reports when available. For task completion, invite a consented task observation rather than treating a visit, click or download as success. Ask the participant how they arrived only within the authorized study. Without attribution evidence report channel as unknown. Do not publish contact details, add query parameters containing writing, add trackers or send messages as part of preparing this protocol.

Decide in advance what would justify another small experiment: qualified recurring workflow, completed real task, manageable support and voluntary return. Compare channel denominators honestly. Low traffic is inconclusive; clicks alone do not validate utility.

## Four-week return, original item 19

Begin the four-week clock only after the participant's first actual article task. Record the first date and permitted follow-up arrangement. A return counts only when a participant voluntarily uses a second distinct real article. A repeat fixture, stated intention, prompted test task or researcher-completed article is not organic return. Record prompted and unprompted activity separately.

The original acceptance goal is at least five initial testers returning with another real article during four weeks. Keep the original enrolled denominator, eligibility, withdrawals, completed follow-ups and missing outcomes visible. No fabricated backdating or assumed return. Record volunteered non-return reasons using participants' words without implying that missing responses explain themselves. Do not send reminders or contact anyone without explicit instructions.

## Paid proposition, original item 20

Candidate hypotheses are reusable agency presets, bounded batch preparation and a future connected handoff. Existing free profiles and collections already cover some setup needs, so first ask which remaining recurring task is valuable and how it is handled today. Do not build a speculative paid integration before observing that gap.

An owner-led, explicitly authorized conversation can test a concrete offer: specified task, input limits, deliverable, turnaround/support, privacy handling, exclusions and an owner-chosen price. Record whether the person has purchasing authority and what they actually committed to. Interest, survey preference, a hypothetical budget and a signed paid-pilot commitment are different outcomes. The earlier suggested target of three to five paying pilots remains unvalidated.

Do not take payment, make delivery promises, activate an AI provider, connect a CMS, buy infrastructure or incur fees as part of this protocol. Check actual demand and feasible delivery before choosing a paid implementation. Until then keep public availability and pricing claims accurate: the current public local tools are free and the private pilot is separate.

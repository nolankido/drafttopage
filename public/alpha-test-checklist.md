## Keeping work, using fictional writing

- Confirm that recovery is off before opting in.
- Enable recovery, edit a sentence, and check its saved status.
- Refresh and confirm that writing is not restored automatically.
- Restore deliberately and confirm that approval is cleared.
- If the candidate offers saved/current comparison, inspect all changed fields, including fields becoming empty, before acknowledging restoration. Changing current writing must require a fresh review.
- Try a second tab while the first is saving. Confirm that it cannot overwrite the copy.
- Pause saving and confirm that the copy remains available.
- Remove the copy deliberately. Confirm that the open article is unchanged.
- Download a private backup, then select it to preview without restoring. Confirm that current writing and approval stay unchanged.
- Inspect its saved writing and notes, then cancel. Choose Restore this backup separately, cancel replacement once, and confirm current writing is kept.
- Restore deliberately, confirm approval is cleared, and inspect the checkpoint of previous writing.
- Keep a downloaded private backup for anything important.

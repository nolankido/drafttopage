Private pilot / Data use

Your writing, and where it goes.

This is an early-stage, invitation-only test, not a public publishing service. Do not submit sensitive personal data, confidential business information, passwords, or material you lack permission to use.

When you generate a draft

Your notes, selected writing profile, audience, and purpose pass through the Cloudflare-hosted backend and are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. The result returns to your browser. The application does not browse for additional information or automatically publish anything.

Application code does not save notes or draft content to a server database, browser storage, or application logs. That is not a promise of zero retention by infrastructure providers. Cloudflare and Anthropic handle requests under their own applicable policies, agreements, and account settings. Review those terms before using private material.

Sign-in and usage protection

A Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite sign-in cookie lasts up to four hours. Signing out removes it from that browser. Rotating the pilot password invalidates existing sessions. There are no separate customer accounts or individual password-recovery features.

A Cloudflare Durable Object stores shared generation and sign-in counters and timestamps, not the text of your notes or drafts. These counters enforce a small pilot usage limit. Application code includes no analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting-provider operational processing is separate.

Your browser and exports

The editor keeps unsaved writing in the open page. Refreshing, leaving, clearing, or signing out can discard it. There is no server-side draft history, recovery service, or autosave. Clipboard copies and downloaded Markdown files are saved by your device and are outside the application's control.

Exports include article metadata and a draft flag. They omit the source notes and review-question list. Review any Markdown, links, or markup in a safe editor before publishing. This pilot does not guarantee factual accuracy or sanitize output for every publishing platform.

Before inviting anyone else

The owner must verify this data flow, the provider account settings and spending controls, and establish a direct contact route with each invited tester. No public sign-up, payment collection, or marketing mailing list is enabled.

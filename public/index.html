The approach

Less setup. More of your own thinking.

01 / Bring the substance

Start with your notes.

A rough draft, an observation, or an idea you have already thought through. The tool should organize what you provide, not invent a life you have not lived.

02 / Shape the page

Keep a consistent voice.

The pilot uses reusable writing profiles for audience and tone. Custom profiles and persistent personal settings remain future work.

03 / Keep the final say

Review, then export.

The initial goal is an editable article and Markdown export. Facts still need your review. Automatic publishing and independent fact-checking are not part of this first scope.

Small on purpose

A useful workflow before a bigger product.

Draft to Page is an early-stage project for independent website owners. A password-protected pilot implements notes-to-draft generation, human review, and Markdown export after the owner configures access and the AI service.

There are no public accounts, sign-ups, payments, or automatic publishing. Private pilot sign-in is for the owner and invited testers only.

This public page includes no analytics scripts or submission forms. Read how the private pilot handles data.
